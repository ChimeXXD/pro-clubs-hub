## Pattern summary

**What the strongest picks share**
- One focus, everything else dimmed: the current step or value at full contrast, the rest at about 30–50 % (8, 9, 12, 17).
- The value first, in its own type: condensed or tabular figures, the label small and muted (12, 14, 16, 19).
- Value, source and freshness in one block, in words (18, 26, 28).
- A position is drawn, not described: a dot or tick on a track, a labelled baseline, labelled ends (20, 21, 23, 25, 27).
- Changelogs lead with the date and use fixed sections (32, 33, 35, 37).
- Libraries carried by type and colour with a count line (38, 39, 42).

**What to avoid**
- Values inside prose; coloured side stripes; rank colours (red, orange, green read as rarity); photo backgrounds under text; rainbow gradients for non-colour data; crests and kit art; confetti; monospace under 13 px.

**Open questions for the direction board**
1. Dark by default, or follow the system? Every arm's-length pick (12, 16) is dark.
2. One step per screen (11) or the whole recipe in one scroll with focus dimming (8, 9)? This decides how "Continue from step n" works.
3. Does a Sculpt step show its track with the number, or the number alone? Tracks add weight on a 50-step face.
4. Skin Tone: a dot on a track plus "62 % along", or the dot only? Visual Age: a pad diagram only?
5. Era badge: "2007–08" with an en dash (30, 43) or "2008"? Monospace or condensed figures?

## Directions to build on the board

- **A. Scoreboard.** Dark default; one condensed free display face with tabular figures; the value as a huge numeral with the part name a small muted cap ("NOSE / 116") (16, 17); the current step a raised card with the rest dimmed (8, 9, 12); a segmented progress bar (10, 13); near-black, warm white and one signal accent for the current step and the tick; quick, functional motion.
- **B. Almanac.** Light first; a serif display face and monospace for dates, era badges and values (23, 30, 36); FaceCards as serif titles on deep muted colour blocks with a count line (38, 42); version tags and credits as sentences (28, 29); the value as a large monospace figure in a hairline chip (14); quiet fades.
- **C. Instrument panel.** Every value both as a number and on its own drawn scale: Sculpt on a tick ruler with a 50 mark and end labels (20, 21), the default as a baseline tick (23), Skin Tone as a dot on a track (25, 26), Visual Age as a ring on a pad (27), build attributes as FotMob rows (24); neutral greys and one cool accent; the pointer springs once to its place when a step comes into view (a fade under reduced motion).

# Where each motion goes in ProBuilds (28 Nov 2026)

The 30 bookmarked animations from designbookmark.com/motion, placed on the screens they belong to. The PDF (motion-placement.pdf) has a marked screenshot of each page.

## Builds (/builds)

- **4 Rubber dock:** The row of 13 archetype emblems under the “Builds” title, grouped Keeper · Defender · Midfielder · Forward. The lime ring on the picked emblem stretches across to the one you tap next, then snaps round it. *All sizes (on phone the rail scrolls sideways).*
- **1 Swipe deck:** The big Stance card under the rail (left half on desktop, full width on phone). Drag it sideways: it tilts with your finger and flies off, and the next archetype’s card settles in. The side cards stay still. This is the only motion on this card (Chime, 28 Nov). *All sizes; drag on phone, drag or arrows on desktop.*
- **24 Morph tooltip:** Hovering an emblem in the rail shows its name (“Finisher”) in one tooltip that slides from emblem to emblem. *Desktop only.*
- **25 Command button:** The magnifier at the far right of the top bar. It widens into a search field in place. Same top bar on every page. *Tablet and desktop.*

## Archetype page (/builds/finisher)

- **22 Expand card:** Each build card in the grid (Meta Finisher, Tall Finisher, Quick Finisher). Tapping a card grows it into the build page, its picture and name carrying across to the page header. *All sizes.*

## Build page: summary column (/builds/finisher/meta-finisher)

- **12 Number stepper:** “Your level − 55 +” under Share build. The 55 rolls up or down on each tap. *All sizes.*
- **16 Exposure slider:** The level track under it: a tick for every level, a taller tick at 50 (where the specialization unlocks), and the knob snaps to whole levels. *All sizes.*
- **14 Storage meter:** The line “AP at level 55: 1,419 · spent 1,410 · left 9” gains a thin bar split into spent and left. *All sizes.*
- **13 Stat card:** The PAC · SHO · PAS · DRI · DEF · PHY row under the Finisher card. The numbers count to their new values when the level changes; the gauges in the Attributes groups below do the same. *All sizes.*
- **24 Morph tooltip:** The PlayStyles and PlayStyles+ grids in the right column. Hovering a badge says what it does. *Desktop only.*

## Build page: Share build sheet (Build page → Share build)

- **3 Parallax ticket (card flip):** One share card in place of today’s two side by side. Its front is the Overview (today’s 1 / 2), its back the Attributes (today’s 2 / 2). Tap or click it and it turns over to the other side, tilting a little as it turns. Under it a hint reads “Tap to flip”, and the card keeps its “1 / 2” count. “Save images” still saves both sides as two images. *Phone: bottom sheet · tablet and desktop: dialog.*
- **19 Loading to success:** The “Save images” button: spinner while the images are made, a check when saved, then back to the label. *All sizes.*
- **20 Copy button:** The “Copy link” button: the copy icon turns into a check for two seconds. *All sizes.*

## Faces library (/faces)

- **6 Tab pill:** The kind chips under the search field (All 5 · Footballers 3 · Athletes 1 · Musicians 1). One lime pill slides to the chip you pick. *All sizes.*
- **8 Smooth tab:** The poster grid below the chips. When the kind changes, the old faces fade out and the new ones slide in from the side of the chip you picked. *All sizes.*

## Face page (/faces/cristiano-ronaldo-2008)

- **18 Scrubber:** The face picture with the “360°” badge (left column on desktop, top of the page on phone). Drag across it to turn the head; a flick keeps it turning and it settles on a frame. *All sizes.*
- **9 Step progress:** The segmented bar under “Step 1 of 18 · Preset”. Each section fills as its steps are done. *All sizes.*
- **11 Task list:** The “All steps” list, opened from the “All steps” link at the right end of the bar: sections with their ticks and a done count. *Phone: sheet · desktop: dialog.*
- **23 Morph popover:** The same “All steps” link: on desktop the dialog grows out of the link instead of the middle of the screen. *Tablet and desktop.*
- **10 Checkbox list:** The “Done, next” button on the step card. Pressing it ticks the step (the check draws in); the same tick shows on each row in All steps. *All sizes.*

## Request a face (/faces/request)

- **28 Ghost suggestion:** The “Who do you want?” field. While typing, the rest of a name already asked for appears in grey; Tab or → accepts it. *All sizes.*
- **29 Letter limit:** The same field: a small ring at its right edge fills as the name nears the limit. *All sizes.*
- **19 Loading to success:** The “Request” button under the field: spinner, then a check when the request is sent. *All sizes.*
- **6 Tab pill:** All · Footballers · Athletes · Musicians chips above “Most requested”. *All sizes.*
- **21 Like burst:** The vote button (arrow and count) at the right of each request row: a small lime burst and the count rolls up by one. *All sizes.*

## Submit a build: step 1, Your build (/submit)

- **9 Step progress:** “1 of 4” at the top right (on phone, in the step line above the title). It fills as you move through Your build → Attributes → PlayStyles → Name and share. *All sizes.*
- **7 Segmented control:** The Keeper · Defender · Midfielder · Forward chips over the archetype tiles. The pill slides and the tiles below fade in. *All sizes.*
- **12 Number stepper:** The − and + beside the big level number (44). The number rolls. *All sizes.*
- **16 Exposure slider:** The level track under the number, with level ticks. *All sizes.*
- **15 Range slider:** The Height and weight card: both sliders, with the value riding the knob and the “Finisher range” ends. *All sizes.*

## Submit a build: step 2, Attributes (/submit (Attributes))

- **14 Storage meter:** The AP counter in the sticky header: a bar of what’s spent and what’s left. *All sizes.*
- **12 Number stepper:** The − and + on every attribute row (Acceleration, Sprint Speed …). *All sizes; skipped when tapped fast.*
- **17 Star rating:** Skill Moves and Weak Foot stars (side column on desktop, after the groups on phone). The picked star pops and the ones before it fill. *All sizes.*

## Submit a build: step 4, Name and share (/submit (Name and share))

- **29 Letter limit:** The “Name your build” field (40 characters). *All sizes.*
- **27 File drop:** The “Your Pro’s face” slot: it lifts while a picture is dragged over it, and the picture fades in. *All sizes; drag on desktop, tap on phone.*
- **2 Fan cards:** “Your card”, the story card preview. Tapping it lifts it into the share sheet, the back peeking out behind it, where it becomes the flip card. *All sizes.*
- **19 Loading to success:** The “Send for listing” button. *All sizes.*
- **20 Copy button:** “Copy link”. *All sizes.*

## Changelog (/changelog)

- **6 Tab pill:** The filter chips (All · Title updates · Archetypes · Seasons · Site). *All sizes.*
- **22 Expand card:** Each entry: tapping it opens in place, the body growing out of the row. *All sizes.*

## Takedown (/takedown)

- **20 Copy button:** “Copy” on the email card. *All sizes.*

## Search (/search?q=ronaldo)

- **25 Command button:** The top-bar search, opened from any page. *Tablet and desktop.*
- **26 Command palette:** The results grouped Faces and Builds. On desktop they open as a panel under the search field, arrow keys moving one highlight; phone keeps the full page. *Desktop panel · phone page.*

## Every page, on phone (any page, 390 px)

- **5 Bottom bar:** The floating nav at the bottom. The lime pill slides to the tab you tap and its label fades in. *Phone only.*
- **30 Notification stack:** The spot just above the nav where toasts appear (Link copied, Saved, Email copied, Vote didn’t go through). A second toast stacks on the first instead of replacing it. *All sizes (bottom on tablet and desktop).*

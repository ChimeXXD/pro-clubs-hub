Pro Clubs Hub (working title, O-01) · Structure, not UI

Sitemap and user journeys

Every page of the launch site, how the pages link to each other, and the two journeys the site is built around: a player who wants a face for their Pro, and a player who wants a meta build for their archetype. This is the brief a UI designer works from. It says what each page must hold and do, not how it looks.

What the site is

A free, static, phone-first hub for EA SPORTS FC 27 Clubs, led by faces (D-02). Faces are step-by-step recipes for lookalike and era heads, each paired with a build. Builds are one clean card per archetype and position, each tagged with the season and the date it was checked in game (D-05).

Who it's for

Primary: Chime, on an iPhone next to the console, glancing between controller moves. Secondary: Clubs players and squads with the same need, and lookalike creators. Contributors: anyone who submits a face or build (SPEC §Users). Content is made and checked by Chime, in game, on PS5 (D-08, D-17).

At launch (19 or 21 Dec 2026)

40 pages: home, 2 libraries (faces, builds), 5 face pages, 13 build pages, the archetypes index and 13 archetype pages, the changelog, submit, about, takedown and 404. Builds recorded on Season 3, which starts Thu 3 Dec (D-18).

Rules the structure follows

  • Every face, build, archetype and changelog entry has its own URL, so any of them can be shared (FUT.GG's "a URL for everything").
  • Drafts are never built (FORMAT, status). Withdrawn content is not built, listed or linked (CLAUDE.md rule 8); its old URL returns the 404.
  • No accounts, no server. Ticks on a face live on the phone that made them (A-07).
  • Every page carries the notice: "Not endorsed by or affiliated with EA or its licensors. Not endorsed by the people named."

Ways in from outside

Expected, not measured: many visitors will land on a detail page, not on Home: a face link shared on X, TikTok or WhatsApp (each face and build gets its own share card, a Should item), or a search like "2008 Ronaldo Pro Clubs face" or "best ST build FC 27 Clubs". So every detail page must make sense on its own: title, season tag, credit and the way back to its library are all on the page, with no assumed context.

Journey 1: the face seeker

6

Who

A Clubs player who wants their Pro to look like someone: 2008 Ronaldo, 2017 Ronaldo, or a living musician. On a phone, next to the console, in a dim room.

Goal

Rebuild the face in FC 27's editor without pausing and rewinding a YouTube video, then play it with a build that fits the era.

Comes in from

A face link shared on X, TikTok or WhatsApp; a search like "2008 Ronaldo Pro Clubs face"; or Home.

Done when

Every step is ticked, the head in the game matches the preview, and the paired build is set up.

1 · DiscoverLands on Home or a face link/

Sees faces first: name, era, kind, the front picture.

→
2 · NarrowFilters or searches/faces

Footballers, an era, or a name. No match: "Suggest one on Submit."

→
3 · JudgeTurns the head/faces/[slug]

Drag to turn; Front, ¾, Side, In a match. Reads the season tag, credit and limits.

→
4 · StartTaps Start, picks the preset/faces/[slug]

Photo preset number, counted among photo presets only.

→
5 · BuildFollows the tabs, ticks each step/faces/[slug]

Skin, Head, Face, Hair; Sculpt if the recipe has it; Visual Age. The value is the biggest thing.

→
6 · FinishLast tick, then "Done"/faces/[slug]

"Now set up the build": the paired build comes up (SPEC flow 1).

→
7 · PlaySets up the build/builds/[slug]

Joins journey 2 at step 5. May share the face.

Coming back

Closes the tab mid-way, or the console needs the TV. Reopens the face: ticks are still there, "Continue from step 9". If Chime has updated the face since, the ticks start fresh and the page says so (A-07).

Watching the tutorial

When the face followed a creator's tutorial, the page shows its title and channel; one tap loads the video from youtube-nocookie.com. Nothing loads from YouTube before the tap (A-05).

Not there

The face they want doesn't exist yet: Faces' empty state points to Submit, which builds a face recipe and sends it, with its captures, to Chime's review queue (open question 1).

Order of the steps (FORMAT)

Preset, then the part tabs (Skin, Head, Face, Hair; only sections changed from the preset), then Sculpt when the recipe has it, then Visual Age. Sculpting a region turns its part into "Custom <part>", which is why parts come first.

Quick and full faces

A quick face uses only the preset, parts and Visual Age, about 20 values (RESEARCH §9). A full face adds Sculpt: dozens of sliders. The recipe decides which it is (sculpt may be empty); every listed step counts toward progress and "Done".

Proposal: "Faces that pair this build" leads into journey 1.

Next season

A patch or new season lands. The changelog entry says what EA changed (linked to EA's post) and which builds were re-checked. A build not yet re-checked shows "Made for Season 2. Values may have changed since." (D-05).

Not a planner

The site gives finished builds, not a tool to spend points. A build planner waits until Chime has checked the cost tables in game (D-07). Players who want to plan go to Pro Clubs HQ or PRO11; we don't compete there (D-03).

Their build isn't here

At launch there's one build per archetype (13); builds for every position come after (a Should item). Proposal: an archetype or position with no build offers Submit.

What isn't in the site, and copy still to write

11

Not at launch (SPEC non-goals, PRD "Cut")

  • A build planner (D-07).
  • Accounts, ratings, a request board, comments (D-09, later).
  • Stats, leaderboards or EA match data.
  • Photo-to-slider AI; any image of a real person; any EA art or icons.
  • Ads, paywalls, affiliate links (always free, D-04).
  • Deceased subjects (no Michael Jackson at launch, D-10).
  • Other game modes; a native app.

Should items (cut first if time runs out)

  • Search across faces and builds.
  • Share card per face and build.
  • Builds for every position (launch has one per archetype).
  • Tutorial embeds; a featured creator spot; a form fallback for Submit without GitHub (O-11).

# Research: FC 27 Clubs, the Ultimate Team sites and the Pro Clubs gap

Researched 18 Nov 2026, written up 19 Nov 2026. Every claim links a page that was opened. Pages seen only in search results are marked so. Decisions that follow from this are in `DECISIONS.md`.

**Verdict.** The builds half of the idea exists: Pro Clubs HQ is close to "FUT.GG for Clubs" and launched in August 2026. PRO11 covers FC 27 with a planner too. Nobody has an organised library of lookalike and era faces paired with builds. That is the opening, plus mobile-first design and honest credit.

## 1. How FC 27 Clubs progression works

- **Clubs moved into The Grounds**, a shared world for up to 100 players, reached through a Clubhouse hub ([EA Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-the-grounds-deep-dive)).
- **13 archetypes.** Keepers: Shot Stopper, Sweeper Keeper. Defenders: Progressor, Boss, Marauder. Midfielders: Disruptor, Recycler, Maestro, Creator. Attackers: Spark, Magician, Finisher, Target ([EA Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-the-grounds-deep-dive); groups from [EA archetypes update](https://ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/fc-27-the-grounds-developer-archetypes-update)).
- **Engine (FC 26) is gone; Disruptor is the only new archetype** ([Pro Clubs HQ](https://proclubshq.com/blog/fc27-archetype-changes/), [Skycoach](https://skycoach.gg/blog/ea-fc/articles/fc-27-clubs-guide); FC 26 list on [ProLeague](https://proleague.de/en/news/all-archetypes-in-ea-sports-fc-26)).
- **Launch Signature PlayStyles** ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/ea-fc-27/all-archetypes-explained/)): Shot Stopper Far Reach, Sweeper Keeper Footwork, Progressor Long Ball Pass, Boss Bruiser, Marauder Quick Step, Disruptor Jockey, Recycler Intercept, Maestro Pinged Pass, Creator Incisive Pass, Spark Trickster, Magician Technical, Finisher Low Driven Shot, Target Precision Header.
- **Second Signature PlayStyle from Season 2 (22 Oct)** ([Pro Clubs HQ](https://proclubshq.com/blog/fc27-archetype-changes/)): Deflector, Cross Claimer, Anticipate, Aerial Fortress, Whipped Pass, Relentless, Press Proven, Long Ball Pass, Inventive, Rapid, Finesse Shot, First Touch, Enforcer (same archetype order).
- **New in FC 27:** all archetypes unlocked from the start; free resets; per-attribute respecs; Masteries (levelling one archetype gives permanent boosts to all builds, e.g. Finisher level 10 gives +1 Finishing and +1 Composure); Amps in four tiers, two Standard plus one Signature equipped, tiers 3 and 4 adding PlayStyles, PlayStyles+ and Perks; archetype-targeted AXP consumables; a Season Pass ([EA Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-the-grounds-deep-dive)).
- **Mastery attributes** found: Shot Stopper GK Positioning and GK Reflexes; Sweeper Keeper GK Handling and GK Diving; Progressor Long Passing and Standing Tackle; Boss Aggression and Strength; Marauder Sliding Tackle and Sprint Speed ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/ea-fc-27/how-to-level-up-archetypes/)); Finisher Finishing and Composure; Target Balance and Jumping ([EA Pitch Notes](https://www.ea.com/en/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-the-grounds-deep-dive)). The other six: not found.
- **Level cap: sources disagree.** Pro Clubs HQ: 40 at launch, **55 since 22 Oct**, 962 to **1,419 AP** ([PCHQ](https://proclubshq.com/blog/pro-clubs-level-cap-rise/)). PRO11 uses 1,419 AP at 55 ([PRO11 planner](https://www.pro11.net/tools/build-planner/#a=magician&t=Acceleration%3A90%2CBall%20Control%3A92%2CComposure%3A90%2CAgility%3A88%2CBalance%3A88)). Dexerto says 75 ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/ea-fc-27/how-to-level-up-archetypes/)). EA's October post doesn't state a cap. Treat 55 as likely, unconfirmed by EA (O-02).
- **Unlocks by level** (PCHQ, not confirmed by EA): PlayStyle slots at 5, 15, 40, 55; PlayStyle+ at 20 and 55; Signature Perks at 10 and 45; Masteries at 10, 30, 55 ([PCHQ level rewards](https://proclubshq.com/level-rewards)).
- **Specializations** unlock at attribute targets, e.g. one at 92 and two at 90; EA removed Attacking Positioning from the requirements ([PRO11](https://www.pro11.net/best-magician-build/), [EA](https://ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/fc-27-the-grounds-developer-archetypes-update)).
- **Height and weight** add hidden plus or minus values to attributes. The menu doesn't show the final value, which also sets AcceleRATE. Written for FC 26 ([ProLeague](https://proleague.de/en/news/how-height-and-weight-affect-attributes)).

## 2. The Ultimate Team sites

- **FUT.GG** (Stormstrike, which also runs MUT.GG and FIFA Index): players and prices, SBC solutions and solver, Squad Builder, Evolutions and Evo Lab, Objectives, Live Events, tactics codes, PlayStyles by role, tier lists, card creator, news, GG Club account sync, iOS and Android apps. Data "directly from the game" plus live market tracking. Its "Clubs" pages are real football clubs, not Pro Clubs ([About](https://www.fut.gg/about/), [homepage links](https://www.fut.gg/)).
- **EasySBC:** AI SBC solutions, Meta Ratings, AI Squad Builder, Evolutions, player database, app, community vote percentages on SBCs. Footer: "All EA FC 27 assets are property of EA Sports" ([easysbc.io](https://www.easysbc.io/)).
- **FUTBIN:** live prices, squad and tactic builder, SBCs, Evolution builder, notifications, AI pack scanner. Free app with paid ad removal. Says it isn't affiliated with EA ([futbin.com/premium](https://www.futbin.com/premium)).
- **FUTWIZ:** £12.50 a year for no ads, subscriber Discord, team reviews, giveaways ([futwiz](https://www.futwiz.com/subscribe/FUTWIZ50)).
- **Money:** display ads plus a cheap "remove ads and extras" tier. FUT.GG's and EasySBC's revenue model: not verified.
- **What makes the best ones good:** instant search and filters, a shareable URL for every squad or build, fast updates on content drops, apps with notifications, account sync.

## 4. How builds are made

- **EA doesn't publish caps or AP costs.** Sites take them from the game and cross-check. PRO11 openly uses PCHQ's cost table and a Reddit user's AP-per-level spreadsheet ([PRO11 planner](https://www.pro11.net/tools/build-planner/#a=magician&t=Acceleration%3A90%2CBall%20Control%3A92%2CComposure%3A90%2CAgility%3A88%2CBalance%3A88)). PCHQ's cost-table page was blocked during research and was not checked.
- **Meta sources:** own testing, EA top-club data, community votes, competitive leagues, creators.
- **Fine:** facts (a specialization target) are free; credit and link the source; embed videos; ask before using a full slider list.
- **Not fine:** bulk-copying a build library or cost table; re-posting a creator's face sliders as your own.

## 5. Faces

- **No face codes.** An FC 27 request says players spend about 15 minutes "manually recreating face presets from YouTube tutorials". EA's only reply was a moderator note ([FC 27 thread](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-27-feedback-en/feature-request-code-system-clubs-avatars/13794923)). The FC 26 request had no reply ([FC 26 thread](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-26-feedback-en/fc26-character-creation-sharing-system-face--appearance-codes/13272068)). An FC 24 thread asked for a game face generator back; no EA reply ([FC 24 thread](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-24-game-modes-en/eafc-24-pro-clubs-game-face-generator-/7884796)).
- **PhotoFit:** leaked before launch ([Dexerto leak](https://www.dexerto.com/ea-sports-fc/ea-fc-27-leak-reveals-you-can-scan-yourself-into-the-game-for-first-time-in-a-decade-3394992/)). An EA community manager said it is still in development and scheduled for Season 4 ([EA forum](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-27-general-discussion-en/cranium-photofit/13712988)).
- **The editor:** start from a grid of preset faces, then four tabs: Skin, Head, Face, Hair (hairstyle, eyebrows, facial hair). Street and matchday looks are separate ([FC Tools Hub](https://fctoolshub.com/en/guides/fc27/face-creator-guide), [FIFAUTeam](https://fifauteam.com/fc-27-clubs/)). Whether sliders show numbers: **not verified**. Face2Slider claims "numerical inputs".
- **Era versions** are limited by the hairstyle and beard list. A face is preset plus sliders plus nearest hairstyle plus build. Say when a hairstyle isn't available.

## 6. Rules and risk

- **EA content policy** ([help.ea.com](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/security-and-rules/ea-content-policy/)): no commercial use; no paywalls ("like Patreon"); passive ads allowed; the notice "This [project/website] is not endorsed by or affiliated with EA or its licensors"; don't combine EA logos with your branding or imply endorsement; licensed third-party content in EA games isn't cleared.
- **Names and icons:** every site uses archetype and attribute names as text. Avoid EA art, logos and icons. FUT.GG and EasySBC serve card images from their own CDNs; that is common practice, not permission.
- **Likeness:** not researched in this round. General guidance: names as text, no photos, no "official" framing, no money tied to a person's name. Risk rises with paid use and protective estates (D-10).

## 7. Data

- **No public API.** ProClubsHead and VPG pull EA match data with a server. Archetypes, costs, level rewards, builds and faces are entered by hand into JSON.

## 8. Not verified

Level cap (55 or 75); whether face sliders show numbers; preset and hairstyle counts; traffic for any site; PCHQ's cost table (page blocked); the YouTube channels (search results only); any current r/ProClubs wiki; FUT.GG's and EasySBC's revenue; the exact Season 3 date; whether "Pro Clubs" is a registered mark.

Brand guidelines

The Grid

A structured identity built on the logic of the game. A slanted P cut into four tiles by the lines of the pitch, set beside a calm, upright wordmark. Clean, system-driven, built to scale across the product.

01 · Logo

Logo system

The horizontal logo is primary. Use the stacked logo where width is tight, and the symbol alone once the brand is already established on the screen (app icon, avatar, favicon, nav on mobile).

02 · Logo usage

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space X = half the symbol height, on every side of the logo. For the symbol alone, X = a quarter of its height. Below 32 px, use the small symbol files, which have wider cuts.

03 · Colour

Pitch, Turf, Floodlight, Chalk, Night, Grass

Dark-first. Pitch carries the surface, Chalk the content, Floodlight the one thing to do next. If a screen has more than one Floodlight element competing, one of them is wrong.

04 · Typography

Inter Display and Inter

One superfamily, two optical sizes. Inter Display (grotesk) for headlines and big numbers, set tight. Inter (neo-grotesk) for everything you read and tap. Tabular figures on for every number. Both are free under the SIL Open Font License.
